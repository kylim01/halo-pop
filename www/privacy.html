Halo Pop Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Halo Pop ("the app") is made by an independent developer based in Singapore. This policy explains what data the app handles.

Data the app stores

Your best score is stored only on your device. The app has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or contacts.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location and information about how you interact with ads, to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads.

If you are in the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. On iPhone, the app asks for permission before tracking. You can change these choices in your device settings at any time, and you can reset your advertising ID there.

In-app purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the purchase is processed by Apple or Google. The app does not see your payment details.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/kylim01/halo-pop/issues.

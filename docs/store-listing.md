## Full description
Halo Pop is a one-tap puzzle you can play in a minute and keep playing for months.

Coloured orbs ride a spinning halo. Tap to launch the orb in the centre into the slot under the arrow. Line up three or more of one colour and they pop. Pop four or more at once and the halo reverses direction, so your timing has to adapt on the fly.

New orbs keep arriving and the halo speeds up as your score climbs. Keep space open, chain your pops into combos and clear the whole ring for a Clear Sky bonus.

• One-tap controls, playable with one thumb
• Swap your current orb with the next one to set up big pops
• Combos, reversals and Clear Sky bonuses
• Short runs, ideal for the MRT or a coffee break
• Plays offline, no account needed

Beat your best score. Then beat it again.
